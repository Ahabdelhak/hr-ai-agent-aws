# Employee Handbook (sample data)

## Vacation
New hires (0 to 2 years of service) receive 15 days of paid vacation per year.
Employees with 3 to 5 years of service receive 20 days per year.
Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 25 days per year.

## Requesting time off
Request time off at least two weeks in advance and obtain manager approval.
Leave requests can be filed through the HR assistant with a start and end date.

## Benefits
Medical benefit claims are reimbursed after submission through the HR assistant.
Keep receipts for every claim. Claims are reviewed by HR within five business days.

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Thursday.
Remote work needs manager approval and is limited to two days per week.
